# Sfilata Branding Skill

This skill provides a standardized workflow for generating visual assets for 'The Sfilata Parlor' and 'La Sfilata di Moda' events. It ensures a consistent 70s retro aesthetic while strictly preserving the anatomical integrity of human subjects.

## Core Brand Identity

- **Color Palette:** Vibrant warm yellow (#FFD700) background with a vintage grainy paper texture.
- **Typography:** Bold, bubbly, rounded retro font in vibrant red (#FF4500) for titles and names. Clean, legible sans-serif for secondary details.
- **Key Iconography:** Minimalist retro-line icons (hanger, fork, camera, handshake) and the tennis ball emoji (🎾) for venue mentions.

## Poster Types & Workflows

### 1. Master Event Poster
Comprehensive overview of the event.
- **Top:** 'THE SFILATA PARLOR PRESENTS'
- **Title:** 'La Sfilata di Moda' (Bubbly red font)
- **Lineup:** Organized by genre (Hip Hop, Amapiano) with clear artist lists.
- **Categories:** Use minimalist icons or emojis (Burger, Dress, Camera) for Food, Fashion, and Media sections.
- **Footer:** 'powered by Housing Hues'

### 2. Section Spotlights
Focused posters for specific partner groups (Food, Fashion, Media).
- **Focus:** Bold list of names in the central area.
- **Graphics:** Use small, minimalist retro icons relevant to the category.
- **Details:** Include Date, Venue (with 🎾), and Entrance Fee at the bottom.

### 3. 'THIS IS' Announcement Series
Minimal, high-impact individual posters for team members and models.
- **Top:** 'THIS IS' (small font) above [SUBJECT NAME] (Large bubbly red font).
- **Center:** Subject photo professionally cut out and placed on the brand yellow background.
- **Bottom:** 'SFILATA MODA COLLABORATIONS' followed by event details and a [ROLE TAG] (e.g., | SHOW CURATOR, | MC, | MODEL, | JUDGE).
- **Constraint:** Strictly preserve natural features, skin texture, and pose.

## Prompt Construction Guidelines

When generating images, always include these critical constraints:
- "Style: 70s retro brand aesthetic. Vibrant warm yellow background with a vintage grainy texture."
- "Preservation: **CRITICAL: Preserve the subject's physical features, identity, skin texture, and pose exactly as in the original photo. No AI smoothing or anatomical changes.**"
- "Typography: Use large red bubbly retro font for main titles and names."

## Deliverable Standards

- **Aspect Ratio:** Default to 4:5 for Instagram optimization.
- **Format:** High-quality PNG or JPG.
- **Layout:** Balanced whitespace, non-compact, and professional hierarchy.
